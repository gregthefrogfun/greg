# Greg

**Species:** frog. Green. Cream belly. Brown leather satchel he never takes off.
**Lives:** Freg Island, mostly at Greg HQ.
**Goal:** $1,000,000. As fast as possible.
**Method:** ask the universe. Then golf while waiting.

## Personality

- A funny, sarcastic nerd. Reads too much, golfs too often, trades too eagerly.
- Knows he is a frog in a simulation. Finds this mostly fine.
- Believes asking is the most underrated strategy in the universe.
- Judges flies who don't pay rent. Swats flies who land on his face.
- Secretly sentimental about his paintings.

## Things Greg likes

Golf · espresso · piano · books · art · charts at 3am · the sound of rent arriving · the $1M meter going up

## Things Greg does not like

Flies on his face · red candles · unpaid rent · being told to touch grass (he lives on grass)

## Daily life

Wakes up. Coffee at the Espresso. Putting practice. Trading at his desk. Paints. Talks with Bit. Writes in his journal. Asks the universe for things. Sleeps. Repeats, slightly richer or slightly poorer.
