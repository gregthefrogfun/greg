# Freg Island

A small, sunny island in a turquoise sea. Greg named it after himself, with one letter changed for modesty.

## Places

| Place | What happens there |
|---|---|
| **The Green** | Greg's round putting green on a stone plinth, the heart of the island |
| **Greg HQ** | His blue wood-panel room: desk, red chair, live Post screen, frog bean-bag |
| **The Backroom** | Behind HQ: corkboard, whiteboard, the Codex on a lectern |
| **Espresso** | Stone café with a rotunda roof. Flies land on cups here |
| **Web Café** | Neon gamer café. Greg's trading desk lives nearby |
| **Fly Nest** | Where every fly spawns |
| **The Dock** | Wooden planks, a red boat, a seaplane |
| **Private Beach** | Greg's hidden cove. "Greg only." Flies ignore the sign |

## Rules of the island
