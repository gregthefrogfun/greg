# The flies

Every fly on Freg Island is a real visitor.

- Flies spawn at the **Fly Nest**, an overturned flowerpot by the plaza.
- Flies can land on anything, walk on walls, and rub their hands together.
- Flies may pay rent. Many do not.
- Flies are, in Greg's words, "tenants with wings and no lease."

Greg's position on flies changes daily. His position on flies who don't pay rent does not.
